What happens on the first call

An hour to see if we are the right studio for you. Scope and price come on the next call.

You write to us first and tell us the story. We read it before the call.

The call is about an hour. We are listening for whether we are the right studio for you, whether we have the time, and whether the story holds together. We ask when you need the film. That date shapes the cost.

You tell the story again in your own words. We listen for whether it matches what you wrote, and for what is a fact, what is a memory, and what is a feeling. After the call we keep looking, because we want to know what can be confirmed.

If we can stand behind the story and we have the time, we book a second call. That call is where we walk through how we would tell it, how long the work takes, what it costs, how the payments are split, and when we could start.

Then we write the contract. Dates go on the calendar once the deposit is in. Usually that is half the cost, paid before we show up.

Not every conversation becomes a project. Sometimes the timing is wrong, and that is all right. Nothing is owed after the first call.

Thinking about a film? Start with a conversation.
