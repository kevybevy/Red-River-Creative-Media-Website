What a shoot day looks like

Three or four hours with you for a bare sitting. A wider film takes more visits.

We recommend a time, and you choose the one that works. The light matters, and so do people's schedules.

For a bare sitting we are there with you three or four hours at most. A wider film is more than one visit, a few hours each. Some of the talking can happen on a call.

Before the day, we have already done a pre-interview. We go over the shape of the conversation with you. If you want the questions ahead of time, we will send them.

On the day we arrive, say hello, and set up while we do. You sit. We put a microphone on you. Then we go through the interview with you. The releases are signed before we roll.

When the interview is done, we make sure the footage is ingested and copied. Then we pack up and leave.

Thinking about a film? Start with a conversation.
