What a family legacy film is

A private film of one life, or of your family. About an hour, built to the budget.

A family legacy film is a private film of one life, or of a family. It runs about an hour, and it stays with your family.

It can start as one sitting. A person talks to the camera, and the research stays light. Sometimes that is all your family needs.

From there, the film can take on more if you want it. More than one place. More than one interview. More people in the family. We scan the photographs. We bring in home video. We look into the family history for you, far enough to be useful, short of a genealogy company. How much of that we do depends on what you want, and on the budget. The scope stays tied to the money.

Thinking about a film? Start with a conversation.
