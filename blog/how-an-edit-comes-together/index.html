How an edit comes together

Your cut, a few rounds of fixes, and a final copy.

We cut the film, then send your family a version to watch.

You tell us what you would like fixed. We make those changes. The contract says how many rounds that is. It is usually a couple.

When the rounds are done, we send you the final copy.

Thinking about a film? Start with a conversation.
