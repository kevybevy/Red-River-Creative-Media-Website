About the studio

RedRiver Creative Media is an independent documentary production company based in The Woodlands, Texas. We are Dru and Janelle, husband and wife, and we have worked together since 2011. We started as reporters. The work began as writing on local issues and investigative stories in Oklahoma and Texas, long before any of it was filmed. The reporting came first. The films followed from that work.

We make documentaries for public release and private family archives, along with commissioned films for organizations and private clients. The work has taken us across Oklahoma, Kansas and Arkansas. Some lives leave behind photographs, letters, a voice on an old recording, and the people who still remember. A family legacy film keeps them in one place, because a life like that should not go unrecorded.

The two of us lead every project. Footage, transcripts and finished films stay private to the family unless the family says otherwise. There is no minimum project size, and the promise is the one on our logo: Media for good, at an honest scope, from a single afternoon of interviews to a multi-year documentary, and media for all.

05 / How it works

  1. talk

    We hear the story and look at the calendar. Then we come back with an honest scope and a number. Nothing is owed after the conversation. There is no minimum project size: a single afternoon of interviews is a project, and so is a multi-year documentary.

  2. prepare and film

    Janelle prepares the interview subjects and leads creative direction. Dru plans logistics and runs the shoot.

  3. edit and deliver

    Dru and Janelle lead the edit and deliver the versions named in the project scope.
